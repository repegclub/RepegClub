Repeg Club — get pubkey from Keplr

Never asks for or touches a mnemonic. Only reads the public key of whichever account is currently active in the Keplr extension.

  1. In the Keplr extension, switch to the account you want (e.g. REPEG-TREASURY-SIGNER-1).
  2. Click the button below and approve the connection.
  3. Copy the pubkey (base64) shown.
  4. Switch account in Keplr and repeat for signer 2 and 3.
